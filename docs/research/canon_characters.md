# Investigación: personajes canónicos, The Final Problem / The Empty House, métodos de Holmes

Documento de investigación para **Books**. Es la fuente de referencia de canon para diseño, narrativa y QA de coherencia.
Época objetivo del juego: **1891–1895** (del "fin" de Holmes en Reichenbach a su regreso y los primeros casos posteriores).

**Dato clave para el diseño.** Entre **dic. 1893** (FINA) y **ago. 1901** (HOUN) no se publicó nada nuevo de Holmes. Un jugador que en 1894–1895 "investiga las publicaciones" tiene como corpus publicado solo esto: STUD, SIGN, las 12 *Adventures* y los 12 relatos de *Memoirs* (CARD solo en la revista). Todo lo demás es "futuro". Para usarlo dentro del juego hay que justificarlo, por ejemplo como manuscritos o notas de Watson sin publicar. Watson menciona que tiene notas: "three massive manuscript volumes which contain our work for the year 1894" (GOLD ✔).

**Coincidencia útil.** Las fechas de publicación dentro del universo coinciden con las reales:
- FINA se escribe "two years" después de 1891, es decir en 1893 ✔.
- EMPT se escribe "at the end of nearly ten years", es decir ~1903 ✔. Watson dice que la prohibición de Holmes "was only withdrawn upon the third of last month" ✔.

⚠ No se verificó si el canon nombra explícitamente a *The Strand* como el medio donde publica Watson. No afirmarlo en el juego sin revisarlo antes.

### 1.3 Cronología interna útil, 1878–1895 (del texto)

- 1878: Watson se gradúa de médico (Universidad de Londres) y va a Netley ✔. El 3-dic-1878 desaparece el capitán Morstan ✔.
- 1880: Maiwand; Watson es herido ✔.
- 1881: Watson conoce a Holmes por Stamford ✔ (STUD).
- 1882 (4 de mayo): Mary Morstan empieza a recibir una perla al año ✔ (SIGN). Coincidencia: el 4 de mayo es también la fecha de Reichenbach.
- 1887: muerte de "Mrs. Stewart, of Lauder"; Holmes sospecha de Moran ✔ (EMPT).
- 1888: SCAN (marzo) y SIGN. Watson se compromete con Mary.
- ~1889: Watson se casa; compra una práctica en Paddington al viejo Mr. Farquhar ✔ (STOC).
- 1890: "only three cases of which I retain any record" ✔ (FINA).
- Invierno de 1890 a primavera de 1891: Holmes trabaja para el gobierno francés; manda notas desde Narbonne y Nîmes ✔.
- 24-abr al 4-may-1891: FINA (detalle día por día en §3).
- Mayo de 1891: Florence; después dos años en el Tíbet (Lhassa), Persia, La Meca, Jartum, y meses en un laboratorio de Montpellier (alquitrán de hulla) ✔ (EMPT).
- 1891–1894: Muere Mary (fecha y causa **no canónicas** ⚠).
- 30-mar-1894: asesinato de Ronald Adair. Abril de 1894: Holmes vuelve y arrestan a Moran ✔.
- 1894: Watson vende su práctica de Kensington a Verner (pariente lejano de Holmes que Holmes financió en secreto) y vuelve a Baker Street ✔ (NORW). Otros casos de 1894: NORW, GOLD (noviembre), Huret.
- 1895: SOLI (abril), BLAC (julio), 3STU, BRUC (noviembre). Casos no contados: Cardinal Tosca, Wilson el entrenador de canarios ✔ (BLAC).

## 2. Fichas de personajes

Formato de cada ficha: **Primera aparición** · **Datos duros** · **Personalidad documentada** · **Habla y tics** · **Qué sabe el público por fecha** · **NO inventar sin justificar** · **Ganchos** (opcional).

### 2.1 Sherlock Holmes

- **Primera aparición:** STUD (nov. 1887). En el universo, 1881.
- **Datos duros**
  - Físico: "rather over six feet, and so excessively lean that he seemed to be considerably taller… his thin, hawk-like nose" (STUD ✔).
  - Familia: "My ancestors were country squires… my grandmother, who was the sister of Vernet, the French artist" (GREE ✔). Mycroft es "Seven years my senior" (GREE ✔). Verner, comprador de la práctica de Watson, es "a distant relation of Holmes" (NORW ✔).
  - Estudios: "the two years I was at college… Bar fencing and boxing I had few athletic tastes" (GLOR ✔). Su único amigo fue Victor Trevor, a quien conoció cuando el bull terrier de Trevor le mordió el tobillo ✔.
  - Oficio: "I'm a consulting detective… I suppose I am the only one in the world" (STUD ✔).
  - Su "career" en 1891: "In over a thousand cases I am not aware that I have ever used my powers upon the wrong side" (FINA ✔).
  - Edad: **no canónica** ⚠. El nacimiento en 1854 es una inferencia de Baring-Gould.
- **Personalidad documentada** (citas)
  - Necesita estímulo: "My mind rebels at stagnation… I abhor the dull routine of existence" (SIGN ✔).
  - Frialdad metodológica: "A client is to me a mere unit,—a factor in a problem. The emotional qualities are antagonistic to clear reasoning" (SIGN ✔).
  - No se casa: "I should never marry myself, lest I bias my judgment" (SIGN ✔).
  - Método: "It is a capital mistake to theorise before one has data. Insensibly one begins to twist facts to suit theories, instead of theories to suit facts" (SCAN ✔). "You see, but you do not observe" (SCAN ✔). "the little things are infinitely the most important" (IDEN ✔). "Data! data! data! I can't make bricks without clay" (COPP ✔). "when you have eliminated the impossible whatever remains, *however improbable*, must be the truth" (SIGN ✔).
  - Sin falsa modestia: "I cannot agree with those who rank modesty among the virtues" (GREE ✔).
  - Rechaza el aplauso público: "His cold and proud nature was always averse… from anything in the shape of public applause" (NORW ✔). Le cede el arresto de Moran a Lestrade (EMPT ✔).
  - Teatral y orgulloso de sus montajes: "the joy and pride which the artist takes in his own creation" (EMPT ✔). "The stage lost a fine actor" (SCAN ✔). Él mismo admite que fue un "unnecessarily dramatic reappearance" (EMPT ✔).
  - Impaciente: "Three years had certainly not smoothed the asperities of his temper or his impatience with a less active intelligence than his own" (EMPT ✔).
  - Alterna pereza y energía: "I am the most incurably lazy devil that ever stood in shoe leather—that is, when the fit is on me" (STUD ✔).
  - Con las mujeres: "a remarkable gentleness and courtesy in his dealings with women. He disliked and distrusted the sex, but he was always a chivalrous opponent" (DYIN ✔).
  - Elige casos por interés, no por dinero: "he frequently refused his help to the powerful and wealthy where the problem made no appeal to his sympathies" (BLAC ✔).
  - Vena contemplativa: "Our highest assurance of the goodness of Providence seems to me to rest in the flowers" (NAVA ✔). En Reichenbach: "Of late I have been tempted to look into the problems furnished by nature" (FINA ✔).
  - **Crítico de las crónicas de Watson.** Es la base canónica de "Holmes sabe que lo investigamos por las publicaciones y reacciona":
    - "You have attempted to tinge it with romanticism, which produces much the same effect as if you worked a love-story or an elopement into the fifth proposition of Euclid" (SIGN ✔).
    - "you have erred perhaps in attempting to put colour and life into each of your statements" (COPP ✔).
    - "Your fatal habit of looking at everything from the point of view of a story instead of as a scientific exercise" (ABBE ✔).
    - Lee FINA: "In your picturesque account of the matter, which I read with great interest some months later, you assert that the wall was sheer. That was not literally true" (EMPT ✔).
    - **Controla qué se publica.** Prohíbe publicar entre 1894 y 1903 (NORW ✔). "'It can't hurt now,' was Mr. Sherlock Holmes's comment when, for the tenth time in as many years, I asked his leave" (ILLU ✔).
- **Habla y tics**
  - Fórmulas y saludos: "My dear Watson" (constante); "my boy" (MUSG ✔); "Elementary" (CROO ✔, la única vez textual relevante); "Excellent!".
  - Murmura "Hum!" mientras lee fichas: "Born in New Jersey in the year 1858. Contralto—hum!" (SCAN ✔).
  - Postura: "putting his finger-tips together and his elbows upon his knees" (FINA ✔); "joining his finger tips and leaning well back" (VALL ✔); "his thin knees drawn up to his hawk-like nose" (REDH ✔).
  - Reacciones: "a little ejaculation of impatience… tapped rapidly with his fingers upon the wall" (EMPT ✔); "that thin, sibilant note which spoke of intense suppressed excitement" (EMPT ✔). Cuando se aburre, deambula, se encierra y toca el violín.
  - Cita a Shakespeare con ironía: "Journeys end in lovers' meetings" y "age doth not wither nor custom stale my infinite variety" (EMPT ✔). Esto contradice el "Knowledge of Literature.—Nil" de STUD.
  - "Come, Watson, come!… The game is afoot" (ABBE ✔). Es de 1897 y se publicó en 1904 ⚠; usarlo en 1894 es aceptable como muletilla.
- **Qué sabe el público**
  - Hasta jul. 1891: es el detective de STUD y SIGN.
  - Jul. 1891 a dic. 1893: un detective famoso.
  - Dic. 1893: muerto.
  - En el universo, desde 1894: vivo y consultado en "every public case", aunque Watson no publica nada.
- **NO inventar sin justificar**
  - Romance: con Irene "It was not that he felt any emotion akin to love" (SCAN ✔).
  - Que busque crédito público.
  - Crueldad gratuita con clientes.
  - Que mate a sangre fría: "I could not shoot him at sight, or I should myself be in the dock" (EMPT ✔). Aun así viola la ley si la justicia lo pide: CHAS, robo en casa de Milverton.
  - Más familia que Mycroft, Verner y la abuela Vernet.
  - Una edad o un pasado concretos.
  - La cocaína en 1894–95: hay que tratarla como un hábito **en retirada**. En MISS (~1896–97) Watson cuenta que "For years I had gradually weaned him from that drug mania… the fiend was not dead but sleeping" ✔.
- **Saberes según Watson en 1881** ("SHERLOCK HOLMES—his limits", STUD ✔): Literatura, Filosofía y Astronomía: Nil. Política: Feeble. Botánica: Variable (venenos). Geología: práctica. Química: Profound. Anatomía: Accurate, but unsystematic. Literatura sensacionalista: Immense. Violín. "expert singlestick player, boxer, and swordsman". Derecho británico práctico.
  - ⚠ El canon contradice después esta lista: habla de "the obliquity of the ecliptic" (GREE ✔), cita a Shakespeare y a Goethe, y en FIVE Watson recita su propia lista: "self-poisoner by cocaine and tobacco" ✔.
  - Uso de diseño: la lista es la **primera impresión de Watson**, no una verdad. Sirve para pistas del tipo "lo que la crónica dice que Holmes no sabe".

### 2.2 Dr. John H. Watson

- **Primera aparición:** STUD (1887). En el universo, 1881.
- **Datos duros**
  - Carrera militar: "In the year 1878 I took my degree of Doctor of Medicine of the University of London… Netley… Fifth Northumberland Fusiliers… attached to the Berkshires… Maiwand. There I was struck on the shoulder by a Jezail bullet" (STUD ✔). Lo salvó "Murray, my orderly" ✔.
  - Herida: en SIGN aparece en otro lugar: "sat nursing my wounded leg. I had a Jezail bullet through it" ✔. Es una **contradicción** ⚠.
  - Su hermano mayor murió alcoholizado; Holmes lo deduce del reloj (SIGN ✔).
  - Prácticas médicas: Paddington, comprada a Farquhar (STOC ✔). En 1891, una casa con jardín trasero cuyo muro "leads into Mortimer Street" (FINA ✔; ⚠ la ubicación se infiere). En 1894, Kensington ("retraced my steps to Kensington", EMPT ✔). En 1894 vende esa práctica a Verner (NORW ✔).
  - Manías: "I keep a bull pup… I object to rows because my nerves are shaken, and I get up at all sorts of ungodly hours, and I am extremely lazy" (STUD ✔).
  - Mary lo llama "**James**": "should you rather that I sent James off to bed?" (TWIS ✔). Inconsistencia famosa ⚠.
- **Personalidad documentada**
  - Leal y valiente. Es "an old campaigner as well as an old friend" (FINA ✔).
  - Emotivo: "I must have fainted for the first and the last time in my life" (EMPT ✔).
  - Aplica el método de Holmes "though with indifferent success" (EMPT ✔).
  - Se describe como narrador torpe: "In an incoherent and… entirely inadequate fashion" (FINA ✔).
  - Su juicio sobre Holmes: "the best and the wisest man whom I have ever known" (FINA ✔).
  - **Altera detalles a propósito por discreción:** "I will endeavour, in my statement, to avoid such terms as would serve to limit the events to any particular place, or give a clue as to the people concerned" (3STU ✔). Es la base canónica para "las crónicas contienen ofuscaciones intencionales".
- **Habla:** "Good heavens!", "My dear Holmes!", "By Jove!" (STUD ✔), "Good gracious, Holmes!" (BLAC ✔). Holmes sobre él: "I am lost without my Boswell" (SCAN ✔). Dos frases posteriores, ⚠ futuras para 1894: "you are a conductor of light" (HOUN ✔) y "the one fixed point in a changing age" (LAST ✔).
- **Qué sabe el público:** todo lo que Watson publica sobre sí mismo.
  - Ignora el nombre de Moran hasta abril de 1894 ("Have you heard the name?" / "No, I have not." EMPT ✔).
  - Según FINA, Watson nunca había oído hablar de Moriarty en 1891 ("You have probably never heard of Professor Moriarty?" / "Never." ✔). VALL lo contradice (§5).
- **NO inventar sin justificar:** a un Watson manipulador o mentiroso deliberado. Lo que sí es canónico: errores (fechas, nombres, la herida) y omisiones por discreción o por orden de Holmes. Un mecánico de "narrador no confiable" tiene que apoyarse en eso.

### 2.3 Mary Morstan (Mrs. Watson)

- **Primera aparición:** SIGN (feb. 1890). En el universo, 1888.
- **Datos duros**
  - Origen: "My father was an officer in an Indian regiment… My mother was dead, and I had no relative in England… boarding establishment at Edinburgh" (SIGN ✔). El padre era el capitán Arthur Morstan, desaparecido el 3-dic-1878 ✔.
  - Trabajo: institutriz de Mrs. Cecil Forrester ✔.
  - Desde el 4-may-1882 recibe una perla al año ✔.
- **Descripción y personalidad**
  - "a blonde young lady, small, dainty, well gloved, and dressed in the most perfect taste… a plainness and simplicity about her costume which bore with it a suggestion of limited means… her large blue eyes were singularly spiritual and sympathetic" (SIGN ✔).
  - Entra "with a firm step and an outward composure of manner" ✔.
  - Holmes la elogia: "one of the most charming young ladies I ever met… She had a decided genius that way: witness the way in which she preserved that Agra plan" (SIGN ✔).
  - "Folk who were in grief came to my wife like birds to a lighthouse" (TWIS ✔). Kate Whitney fue su compañera de colegio ✔.
- **Habla:** cálida, práctica, maternal con quien sufre ("Now, you must have some wine and water, and sit here comfortably", TWIS ✔).
- **Estado en 1891–1894**
  - 24-abr-1891: "Is Mrs. Watson in?" / "She is away upon a visit." (FINA ✔).
  - La carta de Holmes de mayo de 1891: "Pray give my greetings to Mrs. Watson" ✔.
  - Abril de 1894: "In some manner he had learned of my own sad bereavement" (EMPT ✔). EMPT no nombra a Mary; que la pérdida sea ella es el consenso ⚠.
- **NO inventar sin justificar**
  - La fecha y la causa de su muerte no son canónicas. Está viva en mayo de 1891 y fallecida en abril de 1894.
  - FIVE (1887) habla de "my wife was on a visit to her mother's" ✔, cuando su madre había muerto. ⚠ Klinger propone una esposa anterior de Watson. Es teoría ✖.
  - Una Mary villana tendría que ser una "personalidad oculta" muy bien justificada, porque el canon la pinta de forma unánime como dulce y lúcida.
  - En BLAN (ene. 1903) Watson "had at that time deserted me for a wife" ✔. Es una segunda esposa sin nombre (⚠ no se trata de Mary).

### 2.4 Mrs. Hudson

- **Primera aparición:** sin nombre en STUD (1887), "the more stately tread of the landlady" ✔. Con nombre en SIGN (1890) ✔.
- **Datos duros**
  - En SCAN Holmes llama a la casera "**Mrs. Turner**" ✔. Error o sustituta ⚠.
  - Cocina: "Her cuisine is a little limited, but she has as good an idea of breakfast as a Scotch-woman" (NAVA ✔). ⚠ No prueba que sea escocesa.
  - Retrato largo (DYIN ✔): "Mrs. Hudson, the landlady of Sherlock Holmes, was a long-suffering woman… his payments were princely… The landlady stood in the deepest awe of him and never dared to interfere with him… She was fond of him, too".
- **En SIGN:** se preocupa por la salud de Holmes. Habla en estilo indirecto y popular: "he's that strange, sir… I ventured to say something to him about cooling medicine, but he turned on me, sir, with such a look" ✔. Ante la llegada de los Irregulars suelta "a wail of expostulation and dismay" ✔.
- **En EMPT** (clave)
  - **No sabía que Holmes vivía.** Él cuenta que llegó a Baker Street y "threw Mrs. Hudson into violent hysterics" ✔.
  - Los cuartos se conservaron "through the supervision of Mycroft Holmes and the immediate care of Mrs. Hudson" ✔.
  - Movió el busto: "Mrs. Hudson has made some change in that figure eight times, or once in every quarter of an hour. She works it from the front, so that her shadow may never be seen" ✔. Y después: "I went to it on my knees, sir, just as you told me" ✔.
  - Ella recoge la bala: "I'm afraid it has spoilt your beautiful bust, for it passed right through the head and flattened itself on the wall. I picked it up from the carpet. Here it is!" ✔
  - "beamed upon us both as we entered" ✔.
- **Habla y tics:** "sir" en cada frase; obediente, concreta, informa hechos. Es temerosa y leal a la vez.
- **NO inventar sin justificar**
  - Nombre de pila: "Martha" es ✖, viene del ensayo de Vincent Starrett de 1934 y de la "Martha" de LAST, que nunca se identifica con ella.
  - Aspecto físico, marido o pasado: no hay datos.
  - Que supiera de la falsa muerte: el canon dice lo contrario.

### 2.5 Mycroft Holmes

- **Primera aparición:** GREE (sep. 1893). Después aparece en FINA (dic. 1893) como cochero, se lo menciona en EMPT (1903) y vuelve en BRUC (1908, ambientada en nov. 1895).
- **Datos duros**
  - Siete años mayor que Sherlock y "has better powers of observation than I" (GREE ✔).
  - "he has no ambition and no energy. He will not even go out of his way to verify his own solutions, and would rather be considered wrong than take the trouble to prove himself right" (GREE ✔).
  - Versión pública de 1893: "audits the books in some of the government departments" ✔. Rutina: "lodges in Pall Mall… walks round the corner into Whitehall every morning… Diogenes Club, which is just opposite his rooms". Horario del club: "from quarter to five to twenty to eight" ✔. Fue "one of the founders" ✔.
  - Físico: "a much larger and stouter man than Sherlock. His body was absolutely corpulent… eyes… of a peculiarly light, watery grey… a broad, fat hand like the flipper of a seal" ✔.
  - Rapé: "Mycroft took snuff from a tortoise-shell box, and brushed away the wandering grains from his coat front with a large, red silk handkerchief" ✔.
  - **Lee las crónicas:** "I hear of Sherlock everywhere since you became his chronicler" (GREE ✔).
  - BRUC (1895, publicado en 1908): "occasionally he *is* the British government… Mycroft draws four hundred and fifty pounds a year… his specialism is omniscience" ✔. Y también: "Mycroft has his rails and he runs on them. His Pall Mall lodgings, the Diogenes Club, Whitehall—that is his cycle. Once, and only once, he has been here" ✔.
- **En FINA y EMPT**
  - Holmes pasa el 24-abr-1891 "at my brother's rooms in Pall Mall" ✔.
  - Mycroft conduce el brougham: "a very massive driver wrapped in a dark cloak" con un "heavy black cloak tipped at the collar with red" ✔. Holmes: "It was my brother Mycroft" ✔.
  - Holmes le entregó todos sus bienes: "I made every disposition of my property before leaving England, and handed it to my brother Mycroft" ✔.
  - Fue el **único confidente** durante el hiato, financió a Holmes y conservó los cuartos (EMPT ✔).
- **Habla:** lacónico, irónico, condescendiente con "Sherlock". Ejemplos: "I thought you might be a little out of your depth" y el telegrama "Must see you over Cadogan West. Coming at once." (BRUC ✔). Remata deducciones al mismo tiempo que su hermano (GREE ✔).
- **Qué sabe el público**
  - 1893: que existe y que es un auditor.
  - Dic. 1893: los lectores atentos saben que fue el cochero.
  - Su verdadero poder no se revela hasta 1908. En 1894–95 nadie fuera del Estado debería saberlo.
- **NO inventar sin justificar**
  - Trabajo de campo o actividad física. La única excepción canónica es el coche de FINA.
  - Visitas a Baker Street. Solo fue "once" antes de nov. 1895, y fue en GREE ("You don't expect such energy from me, do you, Sherlock?" ✔). Cuidar los cuartos durante el hiato fue "supervision", hecha a distancia.
  - Ambición, matrimonio, honores ("will receive neither honour nor title", BRUC ✔).
- **Gancho (inferencia ⚠):** alguien pagó tres años de alquiler de los cuartos de un muerto. En 1891–94 es una pista "pública" de que algo no cierra.

### 2.6 Inspector G. Lestrade

- **Primera aparición:** STUD (1887). La inicial "G." aparece en la firma "—G. Lestrade." de CARD ✔.
- **Descripción**
  - "one little sallow rat-faced, dark-eyed fellow" y "lean and ferret-like as ever" (STUD ✔). "beady eyes" ✔.
  - "A lean, ferret-like man, furtive and sly-looking" (BOSC ✔).
- **Juicios de Holmes**
  - "Gregson is the smartest of the Scotland Yarders; he and Lestrade are the pick of a bad lot. They are both quick and energetic, but conventional—shockingly so. They have their knives into one another, too. They are as jealous as a pair of professional beauties" (STUD ✔).
  - "although he is absolutely devoid of reason, he is as tenacious as a bulldog when he once understands what he has to do… this tenacity which has brought him to the top at Scotland Yard" (CARD ✔).
  - "He is the best of the professionals, I think" (HOUN ✔).
  - Holmes se queja: "Gregson, Lestrade, and Co. will pocket all the credit" (STUD ✔).
- **En 1894**
  - EMPT: "Yes, Mr. Holmes. I took the job myself. It's good to see you back in London, sir." ✔ Holmes lo pincha: "Three undetected murders in one year won't do, Lestrade. But you handled the Molesey Mystery with less than your usual—that's to say, you handled it fairly well." ✔
  - NORW (1894): "You are too many for me when you begin to get on your theories, Mr. Holmes" ✔. "Well, Mr. Holmes, have you proved us to be wrong yet?" ✔. "I am a practical man, Mr. Holmes, and when I have got my evidence I come to my conclusions" ✔. "You are aware that no two thumb-marks are alike?" ✔
- **Habla y tics:** "Mr. Holmes" con tono sardónico; desconfía de las "theories" y defiende los "facts" (Holmes: "the preference to my theories over Lestrade's facts", NORW ✔). Es "grossly triumphant" cuando cree tener razón ✔. Respeta a Holmes en el fondo ("we owe you a good turn at Scotland Yard", NORW ✔).
- **Qué sabe el público:** que es "a well-known detective" (STUD ✔). En 1894 se le atribuye públicamente el arresto de Moran.
- **NO inventar sin justificar:** corrupción, genio, un nombre completo. Tampoco un Lestrade que sepa del hiato: lo recibe como un regreso.

### 2.8 Inspector Stanley Hopkins

- **Primera aparición:** BLAC (mar. 1904; ambientada en julio de 1895). Para el lector de 1894–95, Hopkins **no existe todavía**. En el universo, sí.
- **Descripción:** "an exceedingly alert man, thirty years of age, dressed in a quiet tweed suit, but retaining the erect bearing of one who was accustomed to official uniform… a young police inspector, for whose future Holmes had high hopes, while he in turn professed the admiration and respect of a pupil for the scientific methods of the famous amateur" (BLAC ✔). De ahí se infiere que nació hacia 1865 ⚠.
- **Otras citas**
  - "young Stanley Hopkins, a promising detective, in whose career Holmes had several times shown a very practical interest" (GOLD, nov. 1894 ✔).
  - Holmes también lo critica: "I am disappointed in Stanley Hopkins. I had hoped for better things from him. One should always look for a possible alternative, and provide against it" (BLAC ✔).
  - "Hopkins has called me in seven times, and on each occasion his summons has been entirely justified" (ABBE, 1897 ✔).
- **Habla y tics:** respetuoso, ansioso por aprender, se frustra rápido ("Stanley Hopkins swore between his teeth, and struck his thigh", BLAC ✔). Lleva libreta.
- **NO inventar sin justificar:** a un Hopkins veterano o despectivo con Holmes.

### 2.9 Wiggins y los Baker Street Irregulars

- **Apariciones canónicas:** STUD (1887) y SIGN (1890). En CROO (1893) aparece otro chico, Simpson. Una búsqueda en todos los libros de Gutenberg confirma que **"Wiggins" solo aparece en STUD y SIGN** ✔. No hay Irregulars en las historias posteriores al hiato ⚠.
- **STUD:** "It's the Baker Street division of the detective police force… half a dozen of the dirtiest and most ragged street Arabs" ✔. Formación: "'Tention!" ✔. Holmes: "In future you shall send up Wiggins alone to report" ✔. Paga: un chelín ✔. "No, sir, we hain't" ✔. "There's more work to be got out of one of those little beggars than out of a dozen of the force… The mere sight of an official-looking person seals men's lips" ✔.
- **SIGN:** "a dozen dirty and ragged little street-Arabs"; Wiggins es "taller and older than the others… with an air of lounging superiority" ✔. Habla: "Got your message, sir… and brought 'em on sharp. Three bob and a tanner for tickets." y "Yes, guv'nor" ✔. Holmes: "The old scale of pay, and a guinea to the boy who finds the boat" ✔ y "They can go everywhere, see everything, overhear every one" ✔. Mrs. Hudson los recibe con un lamento ✔.
- **CROO:** "one of my Baker Street boys mounting guard over him who would stick to him like a burr"; "Good, Simpson!" (Holmes "patting him on the head") ✔.
- **Inferencia ⚠:** Wiggins era niño en 1881 y "mayor" en 1888. En 1894 sería un adolescente tardío o un joven adulto. Volver a usarlo en 1894 es plausible, pero hay que justificarlo.
- **NO inventar sin justificar:** nombres canónicos de otros chicos (solo existen Wiggins y Simpson). Se pueden crear chicos nuevos como personajes nuestros.

### 2.10 Coronel Sebastian Moran

- **Primera aparición:** EMPT (oct. 1903; en el universo, abril de 1894). Se lo menciona en VALL (1914), ILLU (1924) y LAST (1917). No aparece en MAZA (en la obra de teatro *The Crown Diamond* el villano era Moran; en el cuento lo reemplaza el conde Sylvius ⚠, fuente secundaria).
- **Ficha del índice de Holmes, literal (EMPT ✔):**
  > *Moran, Sebastian, Colonel.* Unemployed. Formerly 1st Bangalore Pioneers. Born London, 1840. Son of Sir Augustus Moran, C.B., once British Minister to Persia. Educated Eton and Oxford. Served in Jowaki Campaign, Afghan Campaign, Charasiab (despatches), Sherpur, and Cabul. Author of *Heavy Game of the Western Himalayas* (1881); *Three Months in the Jungle* (1884). Address: Conduit Street. Clubs: The Anglo-Indian, the Tankerville, the Bagatelle Card Club.
  > Al margen, de puño y letra de Holmes: "The second most dangerous man in London."
- **Descripción (EMPT ✔)**
  - "an elderly man, with a thin, projecting nose, a high, bald forehead, and a huge grizzled moustache. An opera hat was pushed to the back of his head, and an evening dress shirt-front gleamed out through his open overcoat. His face was gaunt and swarthy, scored with deep, savage lines."
  - "With the brow of a philosopher above and the jaw of a sensualist below… his cruel blue eyes, with their drooping, cynical lids… the fierce, aggressive nose and the threatening, deep-lined brow."
  - "With his savage eyes and bristling moustache he was wonderfully like a tiger himself."
- **Historia (EMPT ✔)**
  - "the best heavy-game shot that our Eastern Empire has ever produced… your bag of tigers still remains unrivalled".
  - "the story is still told in India how he crawled down a drain after a wounded man-eating tiger".
  - "Without any open scandal, he still made India too hot to hold him. He retired, came to London, and again acquired an evil name… sought out by Professor Moriarty, to whom for a time he was chief of the staff".
  - Mrs. Stewart, of Lauder, 1887.
  - "Moran undoubtedly played foul… The exclusion from his clubs would mean ruin to Moran, who lived by his ill-gotten card-gains."
  - "He was the best shot in India, and I expect that there are few better in London."
- **VALL (✔, ambientada a fines de los 80):** "His chief of staff is Colonel Sebastian Moran, as aloof and guarded and inaccessible to the law as himself… Six thousand a year… It's more than the Prime Minister gets."
- **Habla y tics**
  - Furia contenida y repetitiva: "You fiend! You clever, clever fiend!" / "You cunning, cunning fiend!" ✔.
  - Formalista con la ley: "You may or may not have just cause for arresting me… but at least there can be no reason why I should submit to the gibes of this person. If I am in the hands of the law, let things be done in a legal way." ✔
  - Al disparar: "I heard a little sigh of satisfaction as he cuddled the butt into his shoulder" ✔.
- **Destino**
  - Holmes: "It will be verified or disproved at the trial. Meanwhile, come what may, Colonel Moran will trouble us no more" ✔.
  - En sep. 1902 sigue **vivo**: "the late Professor Moriarty, or than the living Colonel Sebastian Moran" (ILLU ✔).
  - LAST: "Colonel Sebastian Moran has also been known to warble it" (la canción de la venganza) ✔.
  - **El resultado del juicio no es canónico.** Que lo ahorcaran es ✖ (pastiche).
- **Qué sabe el público:** nada antes de abril de 1894. Antes de eso, ni Watson conocía su nombre. Desde abril de 1894 se sabe que lo arrestaron por el caso Adair; el veredicto es desconocido ⚠.
- **NO inventar sin justificar**
  - A un Moran bruto o inculto: es de Eton y Oxford y tiene nervios de hierro.
  - Que lo ahorcaran (choca con ILLU).
  - Su altura o un acento: no hay datos.
- **Gancho:** en el universo, Moran estuvo en Reichenbach tirando rocas (EMPT ✔). Entre 1891 y 1894 fue el último enemigo "en Londres". Holmes leía la prensa criminal esperando su error.

### 2.11 Profesor (James) Moriarty

- **Primera aparición:** FINA (dic. 1893). Personaje central de VALL (1914–15), aunque sin aparecer en escena. Se lo menciona en EMPT, NORW, MISS, ILLU y LAST.
- **Biografía (FINA ✔):** "a man of good birth and excellent education, endowed by nature with a phenomenal mathematical faculty. At the age of twenty-one he wrote a treatise upon the Binomial Theorem, which has had a European vogue. On the strength of it he won the Mathematical Chair at one of our smaller universities… Dark rumours gathered round him in the university town, and eventually he was compelled to resign his chair and to come down to London, where he set up as an Army coach. So much is known to the world".
- **El retrato de Holmes (FINA ✔):** "He is the Napoleon of crime, Watson. He is the organizer of half that is evil and of nearly all that is undetected in this great city… He sits motionless, like a spider in the centre of its web… He does little himself. He only plans."
- **Descripción física (FINA ✔):** "He is extremely tall and thin, his forehead domes out in a white curve, and his two eyes are deeply sunken in his head. He is clean-shaven, pale, and ascetic-looking… His shoulders are rounded from much study, and his face protrudes forward, and is forever slowly oscillating from side to side in a curiously reptilian fashion."
  - EMPT agrega "his grey eyes" y "his long arms" ✔.
  - En VALL MacDonald lo describe así: "He'd have made a grand meenister with his thin face and gray hair and solemn-like way of talking. When he put his hand on my shoulder… it was like a father's blessing" ✔.
- **Habla y tics (FINA ✔)**
  - "His soft, precise fashion of speech leaves a conviction of sincerity which a mere bully could not produce."
  - "You have less frontal development than I should have expected."
  - "It is a dangerous habit to finger loaded firearms in the pocket of one's dressing-gown."
  - "All that I have to say has already crossed your mind."
  - "Tut, tut."
  - "You must drop it, Mr. Holmes… You really must, you know."
  - "It has been an intellectual treat to me to see the way in which you have grappled with this affair."
  - "I will never stand in the dock… If you are clever enough to bring destruction upon me, rest assured that I shall do as much to you."
  - Lleva un **memorandum-book con fechas:** "You crossed my path on the 4th of January… On the 23rd you incommoded me; by the middle of February I was seriously inconvenienced… at the end of March I was absolutely hampered… and now, at the close of April…" ✔.
  - Gestos: "peering and blinking", "swaying his face about" ✔.
- **VALL (✔)**
  - Escribió *The Dynamics of an Asteroid*, "a book which ascends to such rarefied heights of pure mathematics that it is said that there was no man in the scientific press capable of criticizing it".
  - En su estudio tiene un Greuze: "a young woman with her head on her hands, peeping at you sideways". Holmes usa como referencia de precio otro Greuze, "La Jeune Fille à l'Agneau", que en 1865 "fetched one million two hundred thousand francs—more than forty thousand pounds—at the Portalis sale". La cátedra de Moriarty vale "seven hundred a year".
  - "He is unmarried. His younger brother is a station master in the west of England."
  - Paga sus cuentas con cheques "drawn on six different banks".
  - Se lo compara con Jonathan Wild ("fifteen per cent. commission").
  - El informante Porlock ("a nom-de-plume").
  - Explicó un eclipse "with a reflector lantern and a globe".
- **Destino:** cae al Reichenbach el 4-may-1891 según el relato de Holmes (EMPT). "Any attempt at recovering the bodies was absolutely hopeless" (FINA ✔). **No se recuperó el cuerpo.** Los únicos testigos de su caída son Holmes y Moran ⚠ (inferencia).
- **NO inventar sin justificar**
  - Que sobreviva. Solo sirve con una justificación fuerte y si es mecánica, porque el canon lo trata como muerto ("the late Professor Moriarty", EMPT, NORW, ILLU, LAST ✔).
  - Que pelee bien cuerpo a cuerpo: no saca arma y solo intenta abrazar y arrastrar (EMPT ✔).
  - Que lo conozca el público antes de 1891.

### 2.12 Coronel James Moriarty, y el hermano jefe de estación

- **Fuente única:** FINA ✔: "My hand has been forced, however, by the recent letters in which Colonel James Moriarty defends the memory of his brother". Watson llama a esa carta "an absolute perversion of the facts" ✔.
- **Problema canónico** ⚠
  - El Profesor también se llama **James** ("Professor James Moriarty", EMPT ✔).
  - VALL menciona un **"younger brother… station master in the west of England"** ✔.
  - Teorías (✖): dos o tres hermanos, todos llamados James (Starrett, Shaw); un coronel mayor más un menor sin nombre (Klinger); ningún hermano (McQueen).
- **Lo que NO dice el canon:** el contenido de las cartas, su rango real o regimiento, su paradero y su relación con la banda.
  - Esto es un **hueco de diseño muy valioso**. Las cartas del coronel (1893) son una "publicación" del universo que contradice a Watson. Si se las usa, tienen que defender la memoria de su hermano y Watson tiene que poder llamarlas una "perversion of the facts".
- **NO inventar sin justificar:** que el coronel y el profesor sean la misma persona, o que sea un criminal confirmado. Cualquier elección tiene que reconocer que hay dos James.

### 2.13 Parker, "the garroter"

- **Fuente única:** EMPT ✔: "He is a harmless enough fellow, Parker by name, a garroter by trade, and a remarkable performer upon the jew's-harp. I cared nothing for him."
- **Función:** "sentinel" que vigila 221B para la banda. Holmes lo reconoce desde su ventana a la mañana. Avisa a Moran del regreso de Holmes ("I was seen by the sentinel, who would, I knew, direct the colonel's attention to my presence" ✔).
- **NO inventar sin justificar:** a un Parker peligroso o listo ("harmless enough"). Su destino no se conoce. El arpa de boca es un rasgo audible perfecto para un juego.

### 2.14 Ronald Adair y su entorno (EMPT ✔)

- **The Honourable Ronald Adair:** "second son of the Earl of Maynooth, at that time governor of one of the Australian colonies"; "no enemies and no particular vices"; "his habits were quiet and his nature unemotional"; "fond of cards… but never for such stakes as would hurt him… a cautious player, and usually rose a winner".
- **Clubs:** "the Baldwin, the Cavendish, and the Bagatelle card clubs". Ese día jugó whist en el Bagatelle a la tarde y después de cenar.
- **Lady Maynooth:** la madre, "returned from Australia to undergo the operation for cataract". Esa noche estaba fuera con la hija, "spending the evening with a relation". Vuelve a las 23:20 y es quien encuentra la puerta cerrada.
- **Hilda:** la hermana. Vive con ellos en **427 Park Lane** ✔. No tiene más rasgos.
- **El Earl of Maynooth** (padre): gobernador colonial en Australia. Ausente.
- **Miss Edith Woodley, of Carstairs:** ex prometida. El compromiso se rompió "by mutual consent some months before… there was no sign that it had left any very profound feeling behind it".
- **Compañeros de whist esa noche:** "Mr. Murray, Sir John Hardy, and Colonel Moran"; "the game was whist, and that there was a fairly equal fall of the cards. Adair might have lost five pounds".
- **Unas semanas antes:** "in partnership with Colonel Moran, he had actually won as much as four hundred and twenty pounds in a sitting… from Godfrey Milner and Lord Balmoral".
- **La sirvienta** (sin nombre): "She had lit a fire there, and as it smoked she had opened the window". Esto explica la ventana abierta.
- **Easter eggs posibles** ⚠ (coincidencias de nombre en el canon; no hay vínculo explícito):
  - "Murray" también es el ordenanza que salvó a Watson en Maiwand (STUD ✔).
  - "Balmoral" se repite: el "Duke of Balmoral's Iris" de SILV ✔ y el "Duke of Balmoral", padre de Lord St. Simon, en NOBL ✔.
- **NO inventar sin justificar:** personalidades fuertes para Hilda, Lady Maynooth, Murray o Hardy (el canon no da ninguna: hay margen, pero cuidado con contradecir lo de la indagatoria); algún vicio de Adair; otro móvil distinto del que propone Holmes. Ojo: Holmes mismo dice que el móvil es conjetura ("yours is as likely to be correct as mine"), así que es un **espacio legítimo para deducción alternativa** ✔.
- ⚠ 427 Park Lane: según comentaristas, la numeración real de Park Lane nunca llegó tan alto. No se verificó; tratarlo como dirección ficticia.

### 2.15 Von Herder

- **Fuente única:** EMPT ✔: "An admirable and unique weapon… noiseless and of tremendous power: I knew Von Herder, the blind German mechanic, who constructed it to the order of the late Professor Moriarty. For years I have been aware of its existence though I have never before had the opportunity of handling it."
- ⚠ "I knew Von Herder" es ambiguo: puede ser "lo conocía en persona" o "sabía de él".
- **Arma (EMPT ✔)**
  - Parece un bastón ("what appeared to be a stick… it gave a metallic clang").
  - Se arma: "a bulky object… a loud, sharp click".
  - Se bombea con palanca: "a long, whirling, grinding noise, ending once more in a powerful click".
  - Es de retrocarga ("opened it at the breech, put something in, and snapped the breech-lock") y tiene "a curiously misshapen butt".
  - Dispara balas de revólver blandas y expansivas: "who would expect to find such a thing fired from an airgun?".
  - Sonido: "a strange, loud whiz and a long, silvery tinkle of broken glass".
  - Destino: "will embellish the Scotland Yard Museum".
- **Eco posterior** ⚠: MAZA (1921) repite el esquema con un muñeco de cera hecho por "Tavernier, the French modeller" y un fusil de aire de "old Straubenzee's workshop in the Minories" ✔. No es la misma gente.
- **NO inventar sin justificar:** que Von Herder vea o que trabaje para Holmes. Su paradero en 1894 no se conoce.

### 2.16 Oscar Meunier

- **Fuente única:** EMPT ✔: "The credit of the execution is due to Monsieur Oscar Meunier, of Grenoble, who spent some days in doing the moulding. It is a bust in wax. The rest I arranged myself during my visit to Baker Street this afternoon."
- **Inferencia ⚠:** el moldeado llevó "some days" y se hizo en Grenoble. Holmes encargó el señuelo **antes** de volver a Londres. El regreso estaba planeado de antemano, aunque Holmes dice que la noticia de Adair "hastened" su vuelta.
- **El busto:** "a wax-coloured model of my friend, so admirably done that it was a perfect facsimile. It stood on a small pedestal table with an old dressing-gown of Holmes's so draped round it". La bala "passed right through the head" ✔.

### 2.17 Irene Adler

- **Única aparición:** SCAN (jul. 1891; en el universo, marzo de 1888).
- **Ficha del índice:** "Born in New Jersey in the year 1858. Contralto—hum! La Scala, hum! Prima donna Imperial Opera of Warsaw—yes! Retired from operatic stage—ha! Living in London" ✔.
- **Frases clave**
  - "To Sherlock Holmes she is always *the* woman… It was not that he felt any emotion akin to love for Irene Adler… And yet there was but one woman to him, and that woman was **the late Irene Adler, of dubious and questionable memory**" ✔.
  - Su carta: "MY DEAR MR. SHERLOCK HOLMES,—You really did it very well. You took me in completely… I had been warned against you months ago… I have been trained as an actress myself. Male costume is nothing new to me" ✔.
  - Lo saluda disfrazada de joven: "Good-night, Mister Sherlock Holmes." ✔
- **Datos:** se casa con Godfrey Norton, abogado del Temple. Holmes, disfrazado, fue testigo de la boda y la novia le dio un soberano: "I mean to wear it on my watch chain in memory of the occasion" ✔. Holmes pide como pago su fotografía. ## 3. Deep dive: "The Final Problem"

*Strand*, dic. 1893. Los hechos ocurren del 24 de abril al 4 de mayo de 1891. Todo lo que sigue está ✔ en el texto salvo que se marque.

### 3.1 Marco narrativo

- Watson escribe dos años después. Lo empujan "the recent letters in which Colonel James Moriarty defends the memory of his brother".
- Hasta entonces hubo "only three accounts in the public press": el *Journal de Genève* del 6-may-1891, el despacho de Reuter del 7 de mayo y la carta del coronel. Watson: "I alone know the absolute truth of the matter". Es ironía dramática, porque no la sabe.
- Watson ve poco a Holmes desde su casamiento. En 1890 conserva solo "three cases". En el invierno de 1890 y la primavera de 1891 Holmes trabaja para el gobierno francés y le escribe "two notes… from Narbonne and from Nimes".
- Holmes dice que sus casos recientes para "the royal family of Scandinavia, and to the French republic" lo dejaron en condiciones de retirarse.

### 3.3 La carta de Reichenbach, texto completo (dominio público, FINA ✔)

> "My dear Watson,
> I write these few lines through the courtesy of Mr. Moriarty, who awaits my convenience for the final discussion of those questions which lie between us. He has been giving me a sketch of the methods by which he avoided the English police and kept himself informed of our movements. They certainly confirm the very high opinion which I had formed of his abilities. I am pleased to think that I shall be able to free society from any further effects of his presence, though I fear that it is at a cost which will give pain to my friends, and especially, my dear Watson, to you. I have already explained to you, however, that my career had in any case reached its crisis, and that no possible conclusion to it could be more congenial to me than this. Indeed, if I may make a full confession to you, I was quite convinced that the letter from Meiringen was a hoax, and I allowed you to depart on that errand under the persuasion that some development of this sort would follow. Tell Inspector Patterson that the papers which he needs to convict the gang are in pigeonhole M., done up in a blue envelope and inscribed 'Moriarty.' I made every disposition of my property before leaving England, and handed it to my brother Mycroft. Pray give my greetings to Mrs. Watson, and believe me to be, my dear fellow,
> Very sincerely yours,
> Sherlock Holmes."

Cierre de Watson: "An examination by experts leaves little doubt that a personal contest between the two men ended… in their reeling over, locked in each other's arms. Any attempt at recovering the bodies was absolutely hopeless".

## 4. Deep dive: "The Adventure of the Empty House"

*Collier's*, 26-sep-1903; *Strand*, oct. 1903. Los hechos ocurren entre el 30-mar-1894 y abril de 1894. Todo lo que sigue está ✔ en el texto salvo que se marque.

### 4.1 El "Park Lane Mystery" según la indagatoria (lo público)

- Adair vuelve del club "exactly at ten" la noche del **viernes 30 de marzo de 1894**. Muere "between the hours of ten and eleven-twenty".
- Sitting-room: "front room on the second floor". La sirvienta encendió el fuego; como humeaba, abrió la ventana.
- No se oyó nada hasta las 23:20. Puerta "locked on the inside". La forzaron.
- "His head had been horribly mutilated by an expanding revolver bullet, but no weapon of any sort was to be found in the room."
- En la mesa: "two banknotes for ten pounds each and seventeen pounds ten in silver and gold, the money arranged in little piles of varying amount". También "some figures also upon a sheet of paper, with the names of some club friends opposite to them".
- Exterior: caída de "at least twenty feet"; "a bed of crocuses in full bloom… Neither the flowers nor the earth showed any sign of having been disturbed"; ninguna marca en la franja de césped. "no waterpipe".
- "there is a cab stand within a hundred yards of the house. No one had heard a shot."
- "the revolver bullet, which had mushroomed out, as soft-nosed bullets will".
- No falta nada. El veredicto: "willful murder against some person or persons unknown".
- La casa: separada de la calle por "a low wall and railing, the whole not more than five feet high".

### 4.2 El disfraz del viejo librero

- Escena 1, Park Lane, ~18 h. Un grupo de curiosos rodea a "A tall, thin man with coloured glasses, whom I strongly suspected of being a plain-clothes detective". Su identidad **nunca se aclara** ⚠ y es un buen cabo suelto. Watson choca con "an elderly, deformed man" y se le caen los libros. Ve el título ***The Origin of Tree Worship***. El viejo se va "With a snarl of contempt… his curved back and white side-whiskers".
- Escena 2, el estudio de Watson en Kensington. "his sharp, wizened face peering out from a frame of white hair, and his precious volumes, a dozen of them at least, wedged under his right arm"; "a strange, croaking voice". Dice tener "my little bookshop at the corner of Church Street".
- **Los libros que ofrece:** "Here's ***British Birds***, and ***Catullus***, and ***The Holy War***—a bargain, every one of them. With five volumes you could just fill that gap on that second shelf. It looks untidy, does it not, sir?" Con esa frase lo hace darse vuelta. Títulos totales nombrados: 4, contando *The Origin of Tree Worship*.
  - ⚠ Lectura holmesiana no canónica: *The Holy War* sería la obra de Bunyan (alegoría de un asedio), *Catullus* el poeta latino, *British Birds* tal vez Bewick o Yarrell. Son títulos genéricos; Doyle no da autor.
- La revelación: Watson se desmaya ("for the first and the last time in my life"). Despierta con "the tingling after-taste of brandy". Holmes tiene "his flask in his hand".
- Después del disfraz: "the seedy frockcoat of the book merchant"; "a pile of white hair and old books upon the table". "It is no joke when a tall man has to take a foot off his stature for several hours on end."

### 4.3 La versión de Holmes sobre Reichenbach y el hiato (resumen de las citas)

1. "I never was in it." Moriarty lo alcanza en el sendero. Holmes obtiene "his courteous permission to write the short note". Deja la nota "with my cigarette-box and my stick". Moriarty "drew no weapon, but he rushed at me and threw his long arms around me". Holmes: "I have some knowledge, however, of **baritsu**, or the Japanese system of wrestling". Moriarty cae "with a horrible scream" y se ve "struck a rock, bounded off, and splashed into the water".
2. Decisión instantánea: "There were at least three others whose desire for vengeance upon me would only be increased by the death of their leader… if all the world was convinced that I was dead they would take liberties".
3. Huellas: "I might, it is true, have reversed my boots, as I have done on similar occasions, but the sight of three sets of tracks in one direction would certainly have suggested a deception." Por eso trepa: "A few small footholds presented themselves, and there was some indication of a ledge". Llega a "a ledge several feet deep and covered with soft green moss".
4. Ve a Watson y a "all your following" investigar "in the most sympathetic and inefficient manner".
5. Rocas: "A huge rock, falling from above, boomed past me… I saw a man's head against the darkening sky… A confederate". Es Moran. Holmes baja, "landed, torn and bleeding, upon the path". "did ten miles over the mountains in the darkness, and a week later I found myself in Florence".
6. "I had only one confidant—my brother Mycroft"; se lo dijo "in order to obtain the money which I needed".
7. "the trial of the Moriarty gang left two of its most dangerous members, my own most vindictive enemies, at liberty."
8. Itinerario: "two years in Tibet… Lhassa… the head lama… a Norwegian named Sigerson… Persia, looked in at Mecca, and paid a short but interesting visit to the Khalifa at Khartoum the results of which I have communicated to the Foreign Office… some months in a research into the coal-tar derivatives… in a laboratory at Montpellier".
9. "learning that only one of my enemies was now left in London, I was about to return when my movements were hastened by the news of this very remarkable Park Lane Mystery".
10. Llegada: "called in my own person at Baker Street, threw Mrs. Hudson into violent hysterics, and found that Mycroft had preserved my rooms and my papers exactly as they had always been… at two o'clock to-day I found myself in my old armchair".
11. Sobre Mary: "his sympathy was shown in his manner rather than in his words. 'Work is the best antidote to sorrow, my dear Watson'".

### 4.4 Camden House, ruta y trampa

- Ruta a las 21:30, en hansom: "the corner of Cavendish Square… a network of mews and stables… a small road, lined with old, gloomy houses, which led us into Manchester Street, and so to Blandford Street. Here he turned swiftly down a narrow passage, passed through a wooden gate into a deserted yard, and then opened with a key the back door of a house."
- Interior: "bare planking… a wall from which the paper was hanging in ribbons… a long hall… the murky fanlight over the door… turned suddenly to the right… a large, square, empty room… the window was thick with dust".
- "We are in **Camden House**, which stands opposite to our own old quarters."
- El señuelo: la sombra del busto sobre la persiana iluminada, "one of those black silhouettes which our grandparents loved to frame". Mrs. Hudson lo mueve 8 veces en 2 horas.
- La calle: "a bleak and boisterous night". Hay "two men who appeared to be sheltering themselves from the wind in the doorway" (policías). Holmes contaba con que Moran tirara desde la calle, donde esperaba Lestrade: "I did not anticipate that you would yourself make use of this empty house and this convenient front window".
- La captura: Moran entra por atrás, abre la ventana "for half a foot" y dispara. Holmes "sprang like a tiger on to the marksman's back". Watson le pega "with the butt of my revolver". Holmes toca un silbato y entran "two policemen in uniform, with one plain-clothes detective" (Lestrade).
- La metáfora: "Have you not tethered a young kid under a tree, lain above it with your rifle… This empty house is my tree, and you are my tiger… These are my other guns."
- El cargo: Holmes rechaza "the attempted murder of Mr. Sherlock Holmes" y pide el asesinato de Adair. "I do not propose to appear in the matter at all."

### 4.5 El 221B restaurado (inventario visual)

"an unwonted tidiness, but the old landmarks were all in their place. There were the chemical corner and the acid-stained, deal-topped table. There upon a shelf was the row of formidable scrap-books and books of reference which many of our fellow-citizens would have been so glad to burn. The diagrams, the violin-case, and the pipe-rack—even the Persian slipper which contained the tobacco". Después aparecen "the mouse-coloured dressing-gown which he took from his effigy" y el "index of biographies", con la colección de "M".

### 4.6 Móvil (conjetura de Holmes, explícitamente abierta)

"Each may form his own hypothesis upon the present evidence, and yours is as likely to be correct as mine." Su hipótesis: Adair descubrió que Moran hacía trampa y lo amenazó en privado con denunciarlo si no renunciaba a los clubes. Moran vivía de las cartas y lo mató. Adair estaba calculando "how much money he should himself return". Cerró la puerta "lest the ladies should surprise him". Cierre de Holmes: "It will be verified or disproved at the trial."

## 9. Huecos canónicos explotables y prohibiciones

**Huecos donde el canon calla.** Se pueden llenar si no contradicen nada:
1. Fecha y causa de la muerte de Mary (entre mayo de 1891 y abril de 1894).
2. El contenido de las cartas del Coronel James Moriarty (1893).
3. Quién fue el muchacho suizo y adónde fue.
4. Quién escribió la carta falsa de Meiringen; Moriarty es lo más probable ("tall Englishman").
5. El segundo miembro peligroso de la banda que quedó libre (EMPT: "two of its most dangerous members"; en 1894 solo queda uno en Londres).
6. El veredicto del juicio a Moran (está vivo en 1902).
7. El hombre de anteojos de color en Park Lane (EMPT).
8. Quién pagó y cómo se administraron los cuartos de 221B entre 1891 y 1894 (Mycroft supervisa y Mrs. Hudson los cuida).
9. Las actividades de Parker, Von Herder y Meunier después de 1894.
10. Cómo leyó Holmes FINA "some months later".
11. Los casos no contados de 1894–95 (§2.21).

**Prohibiciones de canon.** No hacerlo salvo con una "personalidad oculta" o una mecánica explícita:
- Romance de Holmes. Que Holmes busque fama. Que Mycroft haga trabajo de campo (fuera del coche de FINA).
- Que Mrs. Hudson conociera el secreto del hiato.
- Shinwell Johnson como aliado antes de ~1900. Mercer antes de 1903.
- Que el público de 1894 sepa del "poder" de Mycroft (se revela en 1908).
- Que Moran sea ejecutado. Que Moriarty aparezca vivo sin mecánica que lo justifique.
- Usar Hopkins, Langdale Pike o los casos de *Return* como "publicaciones" en 1894–95. **Existen en el universo, pero no están publicados.**
- Dar como canon "Martha" Hudson, que el profesor naciera en Irlanda, la cátedra en Durham, "Robert Moriarty" u otros elementos ✖.
